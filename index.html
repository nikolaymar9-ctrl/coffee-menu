Espresso-Based Drinks

Espresso – classic strong coffee.
Double Espresso (Doppio) – stronger, double shot.
Ristretto – shorter, more concentrated espresso.
Americano – espresso with hot water, milder taste.
Latte – espresso with plenty of milk, creamy.
Cappuccino – espresso with steamed milk and thick foam.
Macchiato – espresso with a little milk or foam.
Flat White – espresso with a thin layer of microfoam.
Mocha – espresso with cocoa and milk, chocolate flavor.

Iced and Cold Coffee Drinks

Iced Coffee – espresso with cold milk and ice.
Frappe – iced coffee, whipped until frothy.
Iced Latte – cold milk with espresso and ice.
Iced Mocha – cold version of mocha with ice.

Specialty and Flavored Coffee

Caramel Latte – with caramel syrup.
Vanilla Latte – with vanilla syrup.
Cappuccino with Chocolate – classic cappuccino with cocoa.
Coffee with Cinnamon – for a more aromatic flavor.
Irish Coffee – coffee with Irish whiskey and cream (alcoholic).
